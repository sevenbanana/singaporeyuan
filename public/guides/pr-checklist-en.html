Got Singapore PR?
Here's everything that comes next

01 · DO THIS FIRST

Step one isn't at ICA — it's on the CPF website

Many people trip over the order. Once your IPA arrives, submit the health declaration on the CPF website first; everything else queues behind it.

MediShield Life & CareShield Life health declaration

Log in with Singpass and submit on the CPF website. The result takes 3–7 working days to sync to ICA's system — until this light turns green, nothing else can move. Aim to finish it at least 10 days before your ICA appointment date.

Two reassurances: your health does not affect your PR status. If you do have a serious medical history, MediShield Life may add a 30% premium loading (for 10 years) — that is not a rejection; it is how the national scheme stays fair for everyone.

02 · WITHIN 2 MONTHS

Three formalities that put PR truly in your pocket

The IPA is normally valid for 2 months; let it lapse and the approval may be void. Take it in this order:

1

Medical check-up (report valid 3 months)

Do it at an ICA-recognised clinic — usually a chest X-ray and blood tests. Not too early (an expired report must be redone), not too late (leave time for the ICA appointment). Your IPA letter lists the recognised clinics and required tests.

2

Complete the formalities at ICA

Attend your appointment, register your fingerprints and collect the Entry Permit. From that moment your FIN retires, replaced by an NRIC number starting with S/T, and your old work pass is cancelled automatically. Everything starts from the ICA website.
ICA · official PR formalities page →

3

Wait for the blue IC (about 2–4 weeks)

Once the light-blue PR identity card is on its way, start the "identity switch" checklist in the next section.

03 · FIRST MONTH

Your first month with the blue IC: switch everything over

Anything registered under your old pass or passport needs to move to your NRIC. These four come first:

1

Reset Singpass with your NRIC

The old FIN account is retired — every government service now runs on your new Singpass.
Singpass official site →

2

Tell your employer to update your status

Your payslip gains a CPF line (your employer contributes too). The first two years run on graduated rates that step up yearly, reaching the full rate in year three — if you would rather start at full rates, you and your employer can jointly apply.
CPF · contribution rates →

3

Update banks, utilities, telco and insurance

Bank accounts, SP utilities, telcos, every insurance policy… switch each registration to your NRIC, so no claim or payment ever fails an identity match.

4

Convert your foreign driving licence

As a PR you must convert your licence within the Traffic Police deadline — don't wait to be stopped.
Traffic Police e-Services (licence conversion) →

04 · MONEY RULES

New identity, new money rules

The part most people overlook — and the most valuable.

Medical cover: time to re-plan your insurance

MediShield Life starts automatically, with premiums deducted from MediSave. More importantly, you can now use MediSave to buy or upgrade a private Integrated Shield Plan (IP) — the same cover moves from foreigner cash rates to resident rates, and many families re-plan the whole household at this point. For public vs private hospitals and what the bills look like, start with my healthcare guide.

Your tax strategy needs a rerun

Your SRS annual cap drops from $35,700 as a foreigner to $15,300 — but CPF cash top-ups (up to $16,000 in reliefs for yourself + family) now open up to you. If SRS was your main lever, the whole stack needs re-arranging — I've organised 24 tax-saving methods in my tax-saving guide, with top-up links ready to click.

The property bar just dropped — a lot

ABSD on your first home falls from 60% as a foreigner to 5%; and once both spouses are PRs for 3 years, you can buy a resale HDB flat. Time to redo the housing plan.
HDB · flat eligibility official page →

05 · LONG TERM

Three long-term dates for your calendar

PR is not once-and-done. These three decide whether the status survives — and how far it goes.

The REP is your PR lifeline

To travel out of Singapore you must hold a valid Re-Entry Permit (REP) — without one, PR status lapses automatically. It renews every 5 years; renew before it expires, and you can apply online even from overseas.

ICA · REP application / renewal →

Sons in the family? Understand National Service early

Boys granted PR under a parent's application register at 16.5 and enlist around 18 for two years of National Service. Renouncing NS seriously affects the boy's future pass applications and the whole family's immigration record — do this maths before or right after applying for PR.

CMPB · National Service official site →

Next stop: citizenship

After 2 years as a PR you may apply for Singapore citizenship. If that's your direction, start keeping records now — complete tax filings, CPF history and community involvement all speak for you.

ICA · citizenship application official page →
