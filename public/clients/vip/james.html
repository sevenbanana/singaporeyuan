03 · Summary

Your SRS investment, in plain terms

This SRS investment started at the end of September 2025. After the one-time 3% initial charge, S$29,100 was effectively invested into the fund. As of 31 August 2026, the account value has grown to S$30,545.87.

Based on the net amount invested into the fund, the investment has gained approximately 4.97% over 11 months. Compared with the original S$30,000 contribution, the initial charge has been fully recovered and the account is showing a positive return of approximately 1.82%.

The fund also targets an annual distribution rate of approximately 5%. As distributions are automatically reinvested, the investment can be viewed from a longer-term perspective, allowing reinvestment and market performance to compound over time.
